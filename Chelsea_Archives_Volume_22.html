ARCHIVE 01 · CONFIDENTIAL

Chapter I — The Author

Chelsea is the kind of person who doesn't simply exist in a room.
She observes it. She studies it. She quietly takes everything in.

And then, when it comes to me specifically, she serves sarcasm on a silver platter like it's a five-course meal. 🙄

Apparently, I am the chosen recipient of all the sarcasm she has carefully stored throughout the day.

But beneath that beautifully annoying sense of humour is someone incredibly thoughtful, creative, and deeply observant.

She's a poet. A novelist. A psychology graduate. A woman with an entire universe living inside her head.

She thinks outside the box so far that sometimes I'm convinced she forgot there was a box in the first place.

And somehow, I got lucky enough to become the person who gets the PREMIUM SARCASM PACKAGE. No refunds. Apparently. 🙄❤️

Chapter II — The Origin Story

Upper 6 · One FRS social · One bathroom mission

Two girls. One high school. One FRS social. One very unfortunate staircase.

On the way downstairs...

I'm really glad that staircase led you to me. ❤️
ARCHIVE 03 · A NOTE FROM THE HEART

Chapter III — The Friend Who Pushed Me Forward

When our final exams were approaching, you encouraged me to study.

You encouraged me to pray. You encouraged me to fast and seek God for the results we were hoping for.

You didn't just care about the fun parts of our friendship. You cared about where I was going.

That is something I will always remember and appreciate about you.

You reminded me that friendship can be laughter and nonsense, but it can also be encouragement, faith, and wanting to see someone do well.

Some friends make memories with you. The special ones also encourage you toward the person you're becoming. Thank you for being that kind of friend to me. 🤍
